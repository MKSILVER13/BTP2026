ORDER
(Hybrid Mode)

25.09.2025: Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that the date of
default which is apparent from notice recalling loan as well as notice issued
under Section 13(2) of SARFAESI was 30.10.2020, which was within 10A
period whereas in the Part IV, the date of default is mentioned as 31.03.2021
without any basis. The default for which Section 7 application has been filed
against the Corporate Debtor was clearly hit by Section 10A of the IBC, hence,
the application ought not to have been admitted. It is submitted that the said
objection was raised by the Corporate Debtor, which has not been correctly

dealt with by the Adjudicating Authority. Submission needs scrutiny.

Issue notice. Requisites along with process fee be filed within three
days. Let Reply be filed within two weeks. Rejoinder be filed within three

weeks thereafter.
