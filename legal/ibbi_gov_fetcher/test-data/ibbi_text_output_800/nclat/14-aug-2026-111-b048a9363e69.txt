ORDER
(Hybrid Mode)
14.08.2026:
This order is passed in continuation of the order dated
12.08.2026. The Learned Senior Counsel for the Appellant submits they are
actively involved in settling the matter with the Financial Creditor viz. Axis
Bank Ltd. though denied by the Learned Counsel for the Axis Bank Ltd. It is
submitted the issue of actual interest due is pending before the Ld. High Court
of Delhi in W.P. (C) No.6802 of 2026 and the next date given in the said petition
is 28.09.2026.
2.
Learned Counsel for the Respondent however, submits though before
the Ld. High Court they have admitted an amount of Rs.3.80 Crores which
they were to deposit in three installments but if one peruse the impugned
order, it notes in paragraph 25 of the reply filed by the Appellant, the amount
of default was stated to be Rs.4,46,33,447.06/-. Further letter dated
03.08.2026 shown to us today rejects the OTS proposal dated 17.07.2026 and
it shows an amount of Rs.5.10 Crores.

3.
It is the submission of the Learned Senior Counsel for the Appellant to
show his bonafides, the Appellant is inclined to deposit an amount of

Registrar, NCLAT.
4.
Let the amount be deposited in the name of ‘Registrar, National
Company Law Appellate Tribunal, New Delhi’ in an FDR, initially for a period
of six months, but with an automatic renewal clause, within two weeks. In the
meanwhile, as submitted the Appellant would approach the Axis Bank yet
again to find an amicable solution of the issues involved. This order is without
prejudice to the rights of Axis Bank.
5.
List the matter on 06.10.2026.
