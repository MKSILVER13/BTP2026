22.07.2025 -
These Appeals have been filed respectively by the SRA and
the RP challenging the Order of the Adjudicating Authority refusing to approve
the Resolution Plan as approved by the COC or inter alia on the ground that the
COC has not applied its commercial wisdom appropriately.

This Tribunal is also informed that COC too has filed similar Appeal which is
pending now with the Registry.

The quintessence of the issue involved in all three Appeals is that the
Adjudicating Authority has rejected the Resolution Plan which the COC is said
to have approved inter alia on the ground that the COC has not exercised its
wisdom properly.

Learned Counsel for the Appellant takes notice of the Respondent/Liquidator
and the Appellant is required to serve copies of their respective Appeal papers on
the Liquidator.

Post the matter on 4th August, 2025.

In the meantime, in pursuance of the impugned Order, the liquidator shall not
take any further steps.
