O R D E R
(Hybrid Mode)
29.10.2025:
I.A. No. 4389 of 2025: This is an application praying for
condonation of 417 days’ delay in filing the Appeal. The order impugned was
an order passed by the Adjudicating Authority on 04.04.2024 for execution of
order dated 21.11.2023.
2.
Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that the Appellant received
the email from NCLT on 11.05.2025, hence, from the date when email was
received the appeal was filed on 44th day, which is within the condonable
period.
3.
The order impugned was passed on 04.04.2024, which is an order
directing for execution of earlier order dated 21.11.2023. Order dated
21.11.2023 is an order passed under Section 66 of the I&B Code, copy of

[PAGE 2]
Company Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No. 1124 of 2025

2 of 2
which has been filed by the Appellant on record. The order dated 21.11.2023
was an order passed after hearing the Appellant. It is not shown that any
appeal is filed against said order. Appellant, thus, was fully aware of the
proceedings under Section 66 and submission of the Appellant that limitation
for filing appeal against order of execution shall commence only when
Appellant received the email from NCLT cannot be accepted. The limitation
for filing the Appeal commences from the date when order was passed i.e.
04.04.2024, hence, the appeal filed with 417 days delay is clearly beyond the
condonable period. Our jurisdiction to condone delay is limited to only 15
days as per Section 61(2) of the I&B Code, we, thus, are unable to condone
the delay. Delay Condonation Application is rejected. In result, Memo of
Appeal is also rejected.
