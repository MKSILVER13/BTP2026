CASE OF BERREHAB v. THE NETHERLANDS
(Application no. 10730/84)
JUDGMENT
7. Mr. Berrehab, a Moroccan citizen born in Morocco in 1952, was permanently resident in Amsterdam when he applied to the Commission.
AS TO THE LAW
I. ALLEGED VIOLATION OF ARTICLE 8
19. In the applicants' submission, the refusal to grant a new residence permit after the divorce infringed Article 8 of the Convention. They stressed that regular contact with his daughter had been maintained.
20. The Government accepted that Article 8 was applicable. It is not disputed that there was an interference.
21. The Court reiterates that the Contracting States enjoy a certain margin of appreciation in assessing the need for an interference (see Abdulaziz, Cabales and Balkandali v. the United Kingdom, 28 May 1985, § 67). Here, the expulsion order had a basis in the Aliens Act, so it was in accordance with the law.
22. The Court is not convinced that the interference was necessary in a democratic society. A fair balance was not struck between the interests of the community and the applicants' right to family life.
23. There has accordingly been a violation of Article 8.
