O R D E R
(Hybrid Mode)
15.07.2025:
This appeal is filed by the Suspended Director of the
Corporate Debtor, namely, M/s Indian Denim Ltd. against the admission of
the application filed by the operational creditor, namely, M/s Anushri Paper
Pack Pvt. Ltd.
2.
The application under Section 9 was filed by the operational creditor
for the resolution of an amount of Rs. 8,48,127/-.
3.
At the time of preliminary hearing, on 16.10.2023 this court had
passed the following order:

[PAGE 2]
“Issue notice. Notice is accepted by learned counsel
appearing for the IRP. Notice be issued to Respondent
No.1.
Let Reply be filed by the Respondents within three
weeks. Two weeks’ time is allowed to file Rejoinder
Affidavit.
List this Appeal on 06.12.2023.
In the meantime, in the CIRP no further steps shall be
taken.”
4.
The interim order passed by this court dated 16.10.2023 is still
continuing. However, at one stage of the proceedings, the SBI filed an
application bearing IA No. 7139 of 2024 for intervention in the present
appeal, inter alia, on the ground that SBI had advanced a sum of Rs. 26
Crores approximately and in view of therefore, the Corporate Debtor
mortgaged its only immovable property namely, “Factory situated at 145-
147 Village Walthera, Taluka: Dholka, District: Ahmedabad, Gujarat”.
5.
It is submitted by the applicant that since the Corporate Debtor was
not making any payment of the amount due, therefore, a legal notice dated
01.06.2016 was served demanding a total outstanding amount of Rs.
16,52,85,913.09/-.
6.
The applicant has also filed an Original Application bearing O.A. No.
431 of 2016 before the Hon’ble DRT-I, Ahmedabad for a total outstanding
amount of Rs. 17,03,85,072/- but the said application is not making any
headway because of the stay granted by this court on 16.10.2023.

[PAGE 3]
7.
Counsel for the applicant has submitted that the operational creditor
though was served but did not appear in this appeal, therefore, proceeded
against ex-parte on 29.08.2024.
8.
Counsel for the applicant has further submitted that though the case
has been fixed today in the category of Old Cases about which it has been
categorically mentioned that no request for adjournment shall be
entertained but the appellant has not put in appearance and order of stay
which has been passed is not allowing the application filed by the applicant
before the DRT to proceed any further.
9.
Keeping in view the aforesaid facts and circumstances, the present
appeal is hereby adjourned to 08.08.2025 to await the appearance of the
Counsel for the appellant but the order of stay dated 16.10.2023 is hereby
vacated.
