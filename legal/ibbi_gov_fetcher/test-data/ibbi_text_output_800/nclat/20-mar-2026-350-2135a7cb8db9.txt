I.A. No. 1907 of 2026 This is an Application praying for

Delay condoned.
Comp. App. (AT) (Ins) No. 491 of 2026 Heard Ld. Counsel for Appellant and
Ld. Counsel appearing for Respondent.
2.
This Appeal has been filed against the order dated 19/01/2026 by IA
(I.B.C) 5345/MB/2025, filed by the Appellant, has been disposed of.
3.
Appellant, by the I.A. has sought copy of the Consent Terms from the
Respondent.
4.
A statement was made by the Respondent that original consent terms are
not available with them.

[PAGE 2]
2 of 2

5.
Adjudicating Authority, disposed of the Application by making following
observations in Paragraph 3.
“3.
We thus, find no reason to keep the present Interlocutory Application
as prayers made in this Application has already been taken care of, and prayer
for original consent terms could not be granted in view of specific
submissions by the concerned persons that those are not available with
them. Accordingly, the Interlocutory Application bearing IA No. 5345 of
2025 are disposed of.”

6.
We do not find any error in the order passed by the Adjudicating Authority.
However, the submission of the Appellant is that consent term is a part of the
record of the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process proceeding.
7.
It is always open for the Appellant to obtain certified copy of the consent
terms from the record of the CIRP proceeding and use the same wherever
required.
8.
With these observations, we dismiss the Appeal.
