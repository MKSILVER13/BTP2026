It is submitted by the Learned Counsel for the Appellant, which has
not been denied by Learned Counsel for the Respondent Nos. 1,2 and 3 that parties are in
the process of entering into a settlement.

It is further submitted by Learned Counsel for the Appellant that the Rs. 2.25 crore

this Appellate Tribunal.

Having regard to the submission made by Learned Counsel for the Parties, we
permit the parties to bring on record the settlement, along with a joint affidavit, within 3-4
days from today.

Accordingly, list this appeal on 03.08.2026, under the same caption.
