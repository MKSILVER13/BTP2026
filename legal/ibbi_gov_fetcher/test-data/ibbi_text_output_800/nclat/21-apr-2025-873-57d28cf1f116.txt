21.04.2025 :
This appeal has been filed against an order dated
04.04.2025 admitting Section 9 application filed by the respondent herein
who has supplied the printing machines to the corporate debtor.
2.
Learned sr. counsel Mr. Satish Chandra Mishra appearing for the
appellant contends that the MoU was circulated to the respondent which
contained a condition of 12.5% payment advance which payment was never
made. It is submitted that the machine which was installed was under
performance and no satisfactory certificate was issue at any time by the
corporate debtor. It is submitted that demand notice was issued on
08.12.2022 to which a reply dated 10.02.2023 was issued which reply is
notice of dispute and in view of the notice of dispute sent by the corporate
debtor, application ought not to have been admitted.
