O R D E R
(Hybrid Mode)
05.10.2026:
We have heard learned counsel for the Appellant and
perused the record. Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that the
Appellant has submitted a resolution plan, however, the same has been
rejected by the learned Adjudicating Authority.
2.
Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that Appellant being a Flat
Buyer Association of the Corporate Debtor – Astha Buildhome Developers Pvt.
Ltd. has submitted a resolution plan which was approved by the CoC with
voting share of 95.81%, however, the learned Adjudicating Authority on hyper
technical grounds has rejected the said plan and also directed for initiation of
liquidation proceeding.
3.
It is further submitted that liquidation of the Corporate Debtor must be
the last resort and every possible opportunity be explored for resolution of the
Corporate Debtor more so when the same is a real estate company and future
of hundreds of homebuyers is at stake.

4.
Having heard learned counsel for the Appellant and having perused the
record, it is reflected that by passing the impugned order the learned
Adjudicating Authority not only rejected the plan submitted by the Appellant
but has also directed for initiation of liquidation proceeding. We prima facie
are in agreement with the submission made by learned counsel for the
Appellant that the liquidation of the Corporate Debtor should be considered
as last resort when all the hope with regard to the resolution of the Corporate
Debtor is lost.
5.
Having an eye on the fact that the stake of various homebuyers would
be dependent on the fate of the project, in our considered opinion interim
protection be granted to the Appellant.
6.
Issue notice to the Respondents. Steps along with process fee as well
as requisites be taken within three working days from today.
7.
List this appeal on 16.11.2026 under caption ‘for admission (after
notice)’.
8.
It is provided that till the next date of listing the operation of the
impugned order shall remain stayed.
