(Hybrid Mode)
24.03.2026:
This appeal has been filed against order dated 16.03.2026
by which I.A. No.1080/ND/2026 filed by the Appellant has been directed to
be listed on 15.04.2026 and both the parties have been permitted to file their
affidavit on maintainability of the application.
2.
The Appellant’s case is that Appellant by the application has prayed for
refund of the Bank Guarantee and EMD which has yet to be decided by the
Adjudicating Authority. The Appellant had filed a Writ Petition No.3195 of
2026 before the High Court and the High Court has already passed an order
in the Writ Petition where the High Court directed that the Respondent No.1
shall not encash the Bank Guarantee till 17.03.2026 which order was now
extended till 24.03.2026 i.e. today.

3.
As submitted by learned counsel for the Appellant, we are of the view
that protection which was granted by the High Court which is continuing as
on date shall continue till the next date before the Adjudicating Authority. It
shall be open for the Adjudicating Authority to pass further orders with regard
to encashment of Bank Guarantee, on the next date, in accordance with law.
The appeal is disposed of accordingly.
