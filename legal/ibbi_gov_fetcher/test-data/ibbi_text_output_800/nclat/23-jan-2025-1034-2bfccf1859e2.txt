The Respondent No.1 submitted that the terms of the MRA and the Security

affirms that a party can indeed enforce a contract made for its benefit. Therefore,
the Lender's ability to act upon the security documents is valid and enforceable,
irrespective of their direct involvement in the trusteeship agreement.

Findings
43.
We note that application under Section 95 of the Code filed by the
