O R D E R
(Hybrid Mode)

20.08.2026
This appeal is taken up today on an application for early
hearing on the ground the parties have amicably settled their issues. It is
the submission by the order dated 03/07/2026, the Company Petition u/s 9
of the IBC was admitted and Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process had
commenced against the Corporate Debtor - M/s Himaya Apparels Private
Limited. It is submitted the Resolution Professional was appointed, but he
received an order on 13/08/2026 itself and submits he has not received any
claim as yet. It is submitted on 13/07/2026, the matter has been amicably
settled between the Parties and without prejudice to the parties, an amount
of Rs. 65,00,000/- towards full and final settlement of the claims of the
operational creditor was paid.
2.
It is submitted by Ld. Counsel for appellant there was an ongoing
dispute between the parties and the petition u/s 9 ought not to have been
admitted, as is evident from the fact that though in the petition u/s 9, an
