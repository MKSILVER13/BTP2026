u209 p122  [23300-23555) | Deprivation of liberty through imprisonment in default, which was in any event permitted under Article 5 para. 1 (b) (art. 5-1-b) of the Convention, did not belong to the "criminal" sphere (matière pénale) within the meaning of Article 6 (art. 6) thereof.
