O R D E R
(Hybrid Mode)

07.08.2026
Heard Shri Krishnendu Datta, Learned Senior Counsel
appearing for the Appellant, as well as Learned Counsel appearing for the
Respondent Nos. 1 & 2, and perused the records.

Keeping in view the nature of the relief sought and having an eye on the
impugned order that the instant appeal may be disposed of at the admission stage,
issuance of notice to the other Respondents is dispensed with.

Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the Appellant, at the outset, submits
that the Appellant is not having any objection so far as the compliance of the
impugned order dated 06.07.2026, is concerned. However, he apprehends that the
document is of a confidential nature and that, if the same is shared with the
opposite parties having conflict of interest, it may prejudice the interest of
Appellant.

It is further submitted that, in pursuance of the impugned order, he may be
permitted to place the document, i.e., Associate Promoter Support Agreement, in a

[PAGE 2]

close envelope/sealed cover before the Bench for the purpose of perusal by the
Bench and not for being brought on record through an affidavit.

Learned Counsel for the RP is not having any objection so far as the
submissions made by Learned Senior Counsel for the Appellant is concerned.
Learned Counsel for the Respondent No. 1 submits that he is not having any
objection so far as the submission has been advanced by Learned Senior Counsel
for the Appellant, however, he would file an appropriate response before the
Learned Adjudicating Authority.

Perusal of the impugned order would reveal that, by passing the same, the
Learned Adjudicating Authority has directed the Appellant to produce a certified
copy of the “Promotor Support Agreement”, before the Bench for its perusal and
understanding, so that the application, i.e., I.A. No. 369 of 2025, may be
adequately disposed of.
Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the Appellant has fairly submitted
that the Appellant is not having any objection so far as the submission of certified
copy of the “Associated Promoter Support Agreement”, only for the perusal of the
Bench, is concerned, however, having an apprehension that the document may fall
in the hands of the other party which would be detrimental to the interests of the
Appellant.

Keeping in view the apprehension shows by the Appellant and keeping in
view the impugned order, we dispose of the instant appeal with the directions that
the Appellant shall provide a certified copy of the Associated Promoter Support
Agreement in a close envelope to the Learned Adjudicating Authority. Learned
Adjudicating Authority after perusing the same, would place the same on record in
a close envelope/ sealed cover.
With the aforesaid observations, the appeal is finally disposed of.
