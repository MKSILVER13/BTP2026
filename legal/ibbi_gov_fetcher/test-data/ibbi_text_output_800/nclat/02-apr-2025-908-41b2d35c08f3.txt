02.04.2025:
Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that the
Operational Creditor has earlier filed an application under Section 9 on
23.02.2024 which was dismissed by the Adjudicating Authority on
13.04.2024 and thereafter second Section 9 application was filed which was

application is confined to Rs.2.60 Crore based on the invoices which were filed
by the Applicant in the present Section 9 application. Learned counsel for the
Appellant also submits that the Appellant is ready to sit with the Operational
