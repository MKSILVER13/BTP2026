(Hybrid Mode)
10.09.2026:
Having regard to the similarity of issues emerging in both
the aforesaid appeals, these appeals are connected and being disposed of by
passing this common judgment, with consent of the parties.
2.
Company Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No. 1716 of 2026 has been preferred by the
Financial Creditor – HDFC Bank Ltd. and Company Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No.
1717 of 2026 has been preferred by Ms. Sasmita Rani Samanta, Suspended
Director of the Corporate Debtor against the order dated 21.08.2026 passed
by the learned Adjudicating Authority in Intervention Petition No. 6/CB/2026
moved in CP(IB)/32/CB/2025 whereby the intervention application moved by
the Respondent - Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT), who
claimed itself to be an Operational Creditor of the Corporate Debtor, has been
allowed and he has been permitted to intervene in the matter.
3.
Shri Sumesh Dhawan, learned counsel for the Appellant in Company
Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No. 1716 of 2026 submits that it was on 05.02.2026, this
Appellate Tribunal has passed an order in Company Appeal (AT) (Ins.) No.223
of 2026 whereby permission was granted to the Financial Creditor to file an
application under Section 12A of the Code after receipt of the amount with
the further direction to dispose of the application in accordance with law. The
IRP was further directed not to take any further steps till disposal of the
application which will be moved under Section 12A of the Code.
4.
Learned counsel for the Appellant submits that thereafter the dispute
has been settled between the parties and in pursuance of the same, Form FA

11.
Though submissions in detail have been submitted by learned counsel
for the parties with regard to passing of the impugned order, admission of
claim of the Operational Creditor as also time taken by the Adjudicating
Authority in disposing of the application moved by the IRP under Section 12A
of the Code r/w Regulation 30A of the CIRP Regulations, 2016, however, crux
of the mater appears to be that the Appellants before us are aggrieved by the
pendency of the application moved under Section 12A since long. We recall
that by the recent amendment made in Section 12A, which was carried out
on 22.05.2026 w.e.f. 26.05.2026, an amendment has been incorporated in
Sub-section (3) of aforesaid Section 12A, which runs as under:
“(3) The Adjudicating Authority shall pass an order
under sub-section (1) within a period of thirty days
from the date of receipt of the application:
Provided that if the Adjudicating Authority has not
passed an order within such period, it shall record the
reasons for such delay in writing.”.
12.
Though the proceedings before us are prior to aforesaid amendment
carried out in the Section 12A but the intent behind this amendment may not
be ignored by this Appellate Tribunal. When the parties have arrived at an

amicable settlement, it is duty of the Adjudicating Authority and this
Appellate Tribunal as well to facilitate and respect that settlement, however,
within the four corners of the provisions of the Code and regulations provided
therein and also keeping an eye on the law laid down by this Appellate
Tribunal and the Hon’ble Supreme Court.
13.
We are not entering into the procedure which has been derived by the
Hon’ble Supreme Court with regard to the manner by which the Section 12A
application would be disposed by the learned Adjudicating Authority but
would like to highlight that it is the utmost duty of this Appellate Tribunal
and the learned Adjudicating Authority to dispose of the application moved
under Section 12A of the Code with utmost expedition. The real intention
behind aforesaid amendment also appears to be the same. Thus, without
going into the merits and demerits of claims and counter claims and the
submissions made by the learned counsel for the parties, we dispose of
aforesaid appeals with following directions:
(I)
The Operational Creditor - Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology
(KIIT), if has not filed the reply yet, would file the same within four
working days from today with an advance copy to the Appellant
(learned counsel for the Appellant), if the same has not yet been given.
(II)
On receiving advance copy of the reply, the Appellant, if so wish, may
file their response within next three days.
(III)
Learned Adjudicating Authority, after exchange of the pleadings in the
manner indicated above, would proceed to decide application under
Section 12A r/w Regulation 30A of the CIRP Regulations, 2016 as soon
