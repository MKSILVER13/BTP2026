I.A. No. 6053 of 2026 :- This application is for
modification of the order dated 24.08.2026. It is the submission of the Learned
Counsel for the Appellant on 06.01.2026, the Appeal filed against the
impugned order dated 12.12.2025 vide which Section 7 application has been
admitted, was heard by this Tribunal and following order was passed:-
“7. In the meantime, in pursuance of the impugned
order, the Resolution Professional may collate the

2.
An interim order was passed on 06.01.2026 and continued till
24.08.2026 when it was urged the matter has been settled with the Applicant
who filed application under Section 7. It was also submitted the IRP has
received claims worth Rs.600 Crores but as the application under Section 12A
was pending before Learned NCLT, hence, on 24.08.2026, we disposed of this
Appeal with liberty to the Appellant to revive in case application under Section
12A is not allowed by the Learned NCLT. It is the submission of the Learned
Counsel for the Applicant the interim order was not extended till the disposal
of the application under Section 12A as in case the application under Section
12A is dismissed by the Ld. NCLT, the Applicant is to revive this Appeal and it
would be no use to revive this Application if the Appeal become infructuous
by that time.
3.
In this circumstance, though we extend the interim order passed on
06.01.2026 and continued from time to time, till the disposal of the application
under Section 12A of the IBC. We also request the Ld. NCLT that the said
application be considered and be disposed of in the time bound manner since
