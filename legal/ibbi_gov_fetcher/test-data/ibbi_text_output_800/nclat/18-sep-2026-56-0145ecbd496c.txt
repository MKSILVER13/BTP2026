18.09.2026 :
During the course of arguments, it is stated the IRP has
still not been appointed in the present case, who in fact is to become the
Secretary of Apex Court Committee and the Chairman of Project-Wise Court
Committee. We have been informed an application for appointment of the
IRP has been dealt with by the NCLT and IBBI is directed to suggest the
names. Let the said process be complete within a week from today.
2.
Further, we have also been told per paragraph 85(1) of the order dated
06.05.2026, certain timelines were given to the NBCC for start of the project
viz. Condition-I being satisfied on 12.12.2024 itself and Conditions- II, III, V
and VI were to be complied with on or before 31.07.2026. The NBCC was
also required to process the award of work as per Condition-IV prior to
31.07.2026 and complete the award of the contract within one month and
then the construction was to commence w.e.f. 01.10.2026. We have also
gone through the Status Report dated 10.09.2026 filed by the Apex Court
Committee which notes the difficulties being faced by the NBCC in
completion of Conditions- II, III, IV, V and VI. However, the Status Report
dated 10.09.2026 does not state the prospective dates of the completion of
Conditions aforesaid as mentioned in Paragraph 85(1) of the order dated
06.05.2026.
3.

of Apex Court Committee is scheduled for 20.09.2026 wherein the
